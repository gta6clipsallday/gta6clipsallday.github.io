Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

GTA6 Clipper ("the app") is a personal tool that uploads video clips to the user's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the app uses.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • With your permission, the app requests one Google permission: upload videos to your YouTube channel (scope youtube.upload).
  • It uses that permission only to upload clips you choose, with their title, description, tags and thumbnail.
  • It does not read your email, contacts, watch history, subscribers, comments or any other account data.

How data is stored

  • The sign-in token is stored only on your own computer. It is never sent to the app's developer or any third party.
  • The app has no servers and collects no analytics, cookies or personal information.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any user data. Videos are sent only to YouTube, at your request.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at Google security settings. Deleting the token file from your computer also stops all access.
